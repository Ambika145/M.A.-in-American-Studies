Programme Overview

The Master of Arts (M.A.) in American Studies at the Motwani Jadeja Institute for American Studies (MJIAS), O.P. Jindal Global University, is a world-class, fully online programme offering an interdisciplinary exploration of the United States as a political system, cultural force, and global power.

The programme helps students understand:

  • The evolution of American political institutions, governance, and public policy
  • The influence of media, culture, and intellectual traditions in shaping American society
  • Social movements, identity debates, and democratic ideals in contemporary America
  • The United States' role in global diplomacy, economics, and international relations
  • The "Idea of America" as a civilisation shaped by both ideals and contradictions

Through academic research, critical inquiry, and contemporary debates, students develop the analytical perspectives required to interpret the United States within historical, comparative, and global contexts.

What is MJIAS?

The Motwani Jadeja Institute for American Studies (MJIAS) at O.P. Jindal Global University is a premier interdisciplinary centre dedicated to advancing research, education, and dialogue on the United States of America.

Supported by the Motwani Jadeja Family Foundation, the institute honours the legacy of Professor Rajeev Motwani, celebrating his contributions to technology, entrepreneurship, and academic scholarship.

The institute reflects the visionary philanthropy of Ms Asha Jadeja, committed to strengthening education, innovation, democratic values, and collaboration between India and the United States.
